# Vacation Policy

## Annual Leave Entitlement

All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued at a rate of 1.67 days per month. New hires receive prorated leave based on their start date.

Part-time employees receive vacation on a pro-rata basis according to their working hours.

## How to Request Vacation

1. Submit your request through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for vacations of 3 or more consecutive days.
2. Requests for 1-2 days require at least 48 hours notice except in emergencies.
3. Your direct manager must approve all requests before they are confirmed.
4. Once approved, the dates appear on your team calendar automatically.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over to the next calendar year. Days must be used by March 31; any remaining carryover days expire at that point.

## Blackout Periods

Certain teams have blackout periods during which vacation requests are typically declined:

- Finance team: First 5 business days of each quarter
- Customer Success: Last 2 weeks of December
- Engineering on-call rotations: As scheduled per team

## Special Circumstances

Bereavement, medical, and parental leave are governed by separate policies — they do not deduct from your vacation balance. See the Leave Types policy document for details.
