# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## What's Reimbursable

The company reimburses reasonable business expenses incurred during the course of work. Common reimbursable categories:

- Business travel: flights (economy), trains, hotels, ground transportation
- Client meals (with itemized receipt and attendee names)
- Internet stipend for remote workers: up to $50/month
- Approved professional development: courses, books, conferences
- Approved software subscriptions for individual productivity

## What's Not Reimbursable

- Personal travel mixed with business travel (only the business portion qualifies)
- Alcohol beyond moderate amounts at client dinners
- First-class flights for trips under 6 hours
- Personal subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, etc.)
- Office furniture for home offices over $500 without prior approval

## How to Submit

1. Submit all expenses through the expense portal within 30 days of incurring the cost.
2. Attach itemized receipts. Credit card statements alone are not acceptable.
3. Include a one-line justification for each expense.
4. For meals with clients, list each attendee and their company.

Expenses submitted after 60 days require manager approval and a written explanation. Expenses older than 90 days are not eligible for reimbursement.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expenses are processed in the next bi-weekly payroll cycle. Funds typically arrive in your bank account within 5 business days of payroll.

## Pre-Approval

Expenses over $500 require pre-approval from your manager. Travel over $2,000 requires pre-approval from your department head.
