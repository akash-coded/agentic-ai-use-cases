# Return and Refund Policy — Acme ProSeries Headphones

## 30-Day Return Window

You may return your Acme ProSeries headphones within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. The 30-day window starts on the date marked "Delivered" in our shipping system.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a full refund, the product must be:

- Returned in its original packaging
- Free of damage beyond normal use
- Accompanied by the original accessories (charging cable, carrying case, ear tips)

Items returned without packaging or accessories incur a 15% restocking fee.

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log in to your account at shop.example.com
2. Find the order in your Order History
3. Click "Return this item" next to the headphones
4. Choose a reason and print the prepaid return label
5. Drop the package at any major carrier location

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your return.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your product arrives damaged or stops working within the warranty period, this is handled under the warranty policy — not the return policy. See the Warranty Policy document for details.

## Items Outside the 30-Day Window

After 30 days, returns are evaluated case by case and may receive store credit instead of a refund. After 90 days, only warranty-covered repairs or replacements are available.

## International Orders

International customers have a 60-day return window but are responsible for return shipping costs.
