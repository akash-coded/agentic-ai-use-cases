# How-to · Business Analysts

Your differentiator on agent projects is specific: you can produce the **golden set** — the artefact that
turns a fuzzy ambition into something buildable and testable. Nobody else on the team is positioned to.

| How-to | Time | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 📋 **[Build a golden set from real tickets](build-a-golden-set.md)** | 1–2 days | The artefact that *is* the specification |
| 🔀 **[Turn a business process into an agent spec](turn-a-process-into-an-agent-spec.md)** | 2–3 days | A spec engineering can build from |

## The three things to internalise

1. **Expert disagreement is design input, not a blocker.** When two experts disagree about the right
   answer, the case is ambiguous and the correct agent behaviour is **abstention**. Record the
   disagreement as evidence.
2. **The described process is not the real process.** Trace ten real cases. The gap is where the project
   will fail.
3. **Unowned corpus = confident-wrong, eventually.** "Who owns this knowledge and how often does it
   change" is a blocking question, not a nice-to-have.
