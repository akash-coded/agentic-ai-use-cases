# How-to · Solutions Architects

You are hired for judgement under constraint. These are the three constraints that bind most often.

| How-to | Time | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🕸️ **[Choose a multi-agent topology](choose-a-topology.md)** | 1 h | A topology, its H×, and what you rejected |
| 🔍 **[Review someone else's architecture](review-someone-elses-architecture.md)** | 90 min | A scored artefact and a named condition |
| 📊 **[Size and cost a deployment](size-and-cost-a-deployment.md)** | 2 h | A cost model with a break-even point |

## The three things to internalise

1. **Start at one agent and force yourself upward.** Every step up needs a stated reason and a measured H×.
2. **Route the known majority away from the agent.** Usually a 40–70% cost cut, almost always available,
   almost never done first.
3. **Containment before architecture.** In any review, "what can it break and what actually stops it" comes
   before elegance.
