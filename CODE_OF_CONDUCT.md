# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a learning repository, and people asking questions here are, by definition, not experts
yet. Treat them accordingly.

## Expected

- Assume good faith and a genuine attempt
- Answer the question that was asked
- Say when you are unsure — confident wrong answers are worse than none
- Critique the work, not the person

## Not acceptable

- Condescension toward beginners. "Just read the docs" helps nobody.
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Any form of discriminatory language
- Posting others' private information
- Deliberately unhelpful or misleading answers

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/akash-coded/aws-bedrock-agentcore-strands/security/advisories/new)
or contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
